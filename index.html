Does Parchis replace Tally?

No. Parchis feeds Tally and your other accounting software. You keep your books exactly where they are; Parchis only removes the typing.

Do my suppliers need to change anything?

No. They keep sending bills on WhatsApp, email or paper. You connect those channels once.

What happens when a bill is hard to read?

Fields Parchis isn't confident about are flagged for your accountant to confirm. Nothing uncertain is guessed or synced without review.

Can a CA firm keep clients separate?

Yes. Each client has separate books and its own destination, all managed from one account.

Does it handle batch numbers and expiry dates?

Yes. Batch and expiry are captured for every line item, which matters most for pharma distributors and clinics.
